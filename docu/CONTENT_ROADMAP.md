# Strategic Content Roadmap: The Tortilla Knowledge Graph
**Platform:** `tortilladepatatas.org`  
**Vision:** Establish the definitive multilingual culinary knowledge graph, eliminating AI-generated superficiality through archival research, peer-reviewed food science, and regional gastronomy.

---

## 1. Editorial Philosophy & Standards

All published content on `tortilladepatatas.org` must adhere to strict editorial intelligence rules:
1. **Three-Tier Epistemology:**
   - **FACT:** Documented historical records (e.g. 1798 Villanueva de la Serena, 1817 Navarra Cortes) or peer-reviewed scientific studies (CSIC, microbiology).
   - **LEGEND:** Folk narratives and oral histories explicitly designated as folklore (e.g. General Tomás de Zumalacárregui, the anonymous Navarrese farmwife).
   - **OPINION & TRADITION:** Culinary debates, regional stylistic choices, and onion preferences.
2. **Anti-Slop Directive:** Ban generic intros (*"Spain is known for its rich culture..."*), formulaic 3-item bulleted summaries, and non-informative filler text.
3. **Mandatory Bolding:** Bold critical microbiological safety figures: **70°C**, **63°C**, **2 minutes**, **20 seconds**, and **4 hours**.

## 4. Internationalization & Localization Strategy (ES / EN / DE)

Content will not simply be translated; it will be culturally adapted for target markets:

1. **Spanish (`/es` - The Mother Canon):**
   - High technical rigor, historical depth, and cultural nuance (faction disputes, regional debates).
2. **English (`/en` - The Global Standard):**
   - Focus on replacing erroneous tourist recipes (no chorizo in authentic Spanish tortilla!), sourcing European-grade ingredients in North America and the UK, and explaining specific gravity.
3. **German (`/de` - Precision & Sourcing in Central Europe):**
   - Focused on potato varieties available in DACH markets (*Festkochend* vs. *Vorwiegend festkochend* such as *Sieglinde*, *Nicola*, or *Cilena*).
   - Clarifying differences between Spanish Tortilla and Italian Frittata or French folded omelettes.

## 5. Editorial Workflow & Quality Checklist

Before any content node is merged into production, it must pass the following four-point verification:
- [ ] **Factuality Audit:** All historical dates cross-referenced with primary sources.
- [ ] **Food Safety Compliance:** All thermal milestones explicitly stated with bold formatting (**70°C for 2 minutes** or **63°C for 20 seconds**).
- [ ] **Taxonomy Linking:** At least 3 related entities linked (1 ingredient, 1 technique, 1 region/person).
- [ ] **Zero AI Slop:** Scanned for banned adjectives, generic SaaS verbiage, and repetitive three-point summaries.
