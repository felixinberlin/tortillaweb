# Vision & Philosophy — tortilladepatatas.org

> **"The Undisputed Global Digital Sanctuary for the Spanish Omelette (Tortilla de Patatas)."**

`tortilladepatatas.org` is a open-source, multilingual digital encyclopedia, interactive laboratory, and worldstate simulator dedicated entirely to the cultural, historical, culinary, and scientific phenomenon of the **Tortilla de Patatas** (Spanish Omelette).

We are the ONLY platform on the global internet combining culinary rigor, food safety science, interactive ratio engineering, faction sociology, trivia gaming, and real-time worldstate simulation.

---

## Core Pillars & Commitments

### 1. Skeuomorphic-Modernist "Kitchen Notebook" Design Aesthetic
Fusing the physical warmth of traditional Spanish kitchen recipe notebooks with sleek modern web performance:
- **Parchment & Warm Neutrals**: Backgrounds in Frit Potato Cream (`#F5E6BE`) and Parchment (`#FFFDF9`).
- **Brand Accents**: Runny Yolk Gold (`#FFB800`), Caramelized Onion Umber (`#8D6E63`), Pilot Light Blue (`#00A3FF`), Safety Threshold Orange (`#FF8A00`), and Bactericidal Crimson (`#D32F2F`).
- **Parchment Stack Shadows & Card Edges**: Tactile borders, rounded notebook corners, and card textures.

### 2. Food Safety Science & Thermal Bactericidal Rules
Culinary perfection without compromising food safety:
- **Gold Pasteurization Standard**: Core thermal stabilization at **70°C for 2 minutes** guarantees bactericidal destruction of Salmonella while preserving creamy texture.
- **Runny Yolk Threshold**: Controlled pasteurization at **63°C for 20 seconds** for authentic Betanzos-style runny egg dishes.
- **Exposure Limit**: Maximum **4 hours** room-temperature exposure threshold before mandatory refrigeration or disposal.

### 3. Faction Sociology & Great Debates
Acknowledging and celebrating the passion of Spanish Omelette culture:
- **Concebollistas** (Pro-Onion: sweet caramelized onion depth).
- **Sinbollistas / Puristas** (Egg, potato, olive oil, salt — absolute purity).
- **Betanceiros** (Betanzos style: ultra-runny egg yolk, crispy potato chips).
- **Vanguardistas / Innovadores** (Modern haute cuisine variations, siphon foams, low-temperature cooking).

### 4. Interactive Laboratorio & WorldState Simulation
Hands-on tools for culinary enthusiasts, home cooks, and developers:
- **Constructor / Omelette Builder**: Precise ratio calculation for eggs, potatoes, oil absorption, salt per gram, and calorie counts.
- **WorldState Simulator & CLI Console**: Real-time state persistence (`happiness`, `danceCount`, `disappointmentCount`, `flipCount`, `onionStatus`, thermal core checks at **70°C for 2 minutes**) accessible via UI action buttons and embedded terminal CLI (`tortilla dance`, `tortilla disappoint`, `tortilla status`).
- **Style Comparator**: Side-by-side technical matrix comparing viscosity, thermal core, and macronutrients across styles.
- **Trivia Challenge Engine**: 200+ verified historical and scientific questions with scorekeeping and explanations.
