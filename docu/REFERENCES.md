## 1. Historical & Historiographical Manuscripts

### 1.1 The Origin in Villanueva de la Serena (1798)
* **Discovery & Attribution**: Unearthed by CSIC historian and researcher **Javier López Linaje** (2008).
* **Primary Publication**: López Linaje, Javier. *La patata en España: Historia y agroecología del tubérculo andino*. Editorial CSIC, 2008 (pp. 235–248).
* **Historical Document**: Manuscript dated **February 27, 1798**, written by **Joseph de Tena Godoy y Malfeyto** and the **Marqués de Robledo**, published in the *Semanario de Agricultura y Artes Dirigido a los Párrocos* (Madrid, Vol. IV, No. 85, October 1798).
* **Significance**: Documents the earliest documented recipe mixing pan-fried potato slices and beaten eggs to create a nutritious, affordable bread substitute during late 18th-century grain shortages in Extremadura.

### 1.2 The Carlist Legend & Memorial Ratón in Navarra (1817–1835)
* **Primary Document (1817)**: *Memorial de la Diputación Foral de Navarra a las Cortes de Navarra* (1817), known as the *Memorial Ratón*, recording the subsistence food of Navarrese peasants: *"dos o tres huevos en tortilla para cinco o seis personas mezclando patatas, pan, etc."*
* **The Zumalacárregui Legend (1835)**: Oral historiography compiled by 19th-century chroniclers attributing the military adoption of the tortilla to Carlist General **Tomás de Zumalacárregui** during the Siege of Bilbao (1835), prepared by an anonymous Navarrese peasant woman.

### 1.3 Pre-Columbian Egg Tortillas & European Potato Introduction
* **Aztec Chroniclers**: Fray Bernardino de Sahagún, *Historia general de las cosas de la Nueva España* (c. 1577, *Florentine Codex*), documenting *totolin tetl* (poultry egg tortillas) sold in the Tlatelolco market.
* **Potato Introduction to Europe**: Pedro Cieza de León, *Crónica del Perú* (Seville, 1553), recording the Andean *papa* (*Solanum tuberosum*). Early hospital records in Seville (*Hospital de las Cinco Llagas / La Sangre*, 1573) document the first purchase of potatoes as hospital food.

## 2. Food Safety, Microbiology & Legislative Standards

### 2.1 Spanish Food Safety Legislation (BOE / AESAN)
* **Real Decreto 1021/2022, de 13 de diciembre**:
  * *Official Reference*: BOE-A-2022-21443 (*Regula determinados requisitos en materia de higiene de la producción y comercialización de los productos alimenticios en establecimientos de comercio al por menor*).
  * *Critical Thermal Standards*:
    * **70 °C for 2 minutes**: Bactericidal cooking target throughout the food mass.
    * **63 °C for 20 seconds**: Minimum thermal pasteurization threshold for preparations with egg intended for immediate service.
    * **Ambient Exposure Limit**: Maximum **4 hours** holding time at room temperature (> 8 °C to < 60 °C) before requiring refrigerated disposal.
* **Predecessor Legislation**: *Real Decreto 1254/1991, de 2 de agosto*, on microbiological rules for mayonnaise and egg-based foods in catering.

### 2.2 Microbiological Inactivation Kinetics
* **Target Pathogen**: *Salmonella enterica* subspecies *enterica* serovar Enteritidis (PT4 and other clinical strains).
* **Thermal Inactivation Curves ($D$- and $z$-values)**:
  * $D_{60^\circ\text{C}} \approx 0.15\text{–}0.25\text{ min}$ in whole egg matrix (International Commission on Microbiological Specifications for Foods - ICMSF).
  * Achieving $\ge 5\text{ log}_{10}$ (5-log) reduction of viable cells requires holding the core at **70 °C for 2 minutes** or **63 °C for 20 seconds**.
* **Risk Modeling Reference**: FAO/WHO *Risk Ranger* quantitative food safety calculation framework; AESAN Scientific Committee Reports on egg preparation safety in catering (2020, 2022).

## 3. Culinary Physics, Chemistry & Physicochemical Science

### 3.1 Egg Protein Denaturation & Coagulation
* **Primary References**:
  * McGee, Harold. *On Food and Cooking: The Science and Lore of the Kitchen*. Scribner, New York, 2004 (Chapter 2: "Eggs", pp. 68–117).
  * This, Hervé. *Molecular Gastronomy: Exploring the Science of Flavor*. Columbia University Press, 2006 (Section: "The Softness of Egg Gels").
* **Key Denaturation Temperatures**:
  * **Ovotransferrin (Conalbumin)**: Denatures and begins gel network formation at **62 °C – 65 °C**.
  * **Ovalbumin**: Major egg white protein (54% of total albumen), denatures at **80 °C – 84 °C**.
  * **Yolk Lipovitellin & LDL**: Thickens and coagulates between **65 °C and 70 °C**.
  * **Effect of Table Salt (NaCl)**: Dissolves protein bundles, lowering the coagulation threshold and resulting in a smoother, more tender curd matrix without water expulsion (syneresis).

### 3.2 Potato Starch Rheology & Confit Physics
* **Cultivar Agronomy**: Technical reports from the *Instituto Vasco de Investigación y Desarrollo Agrario (NEIKER)* and the *Ministerio de Agricultura, Pesca y Alimentación (MAPA)*:
  * *Monalisa*: 18–20% dry matter, intermediate starch, ideal for poaching without shattering.
  * *Kennebec*: 20–22% dry matter, Galician staple, crispy exterior with tender core.
  * *Agria*: 22–24% dry matter, high starch, lowest oil absorption.
* **Technique of "Chascar" (Snapping)**: Irregular cutting breaks amyloplast cell walls, shedding free amylopectin starch into the warm egg bath during the **5-minute rest**, naturally thickening the emulsion.
* **Lipid Thermal Confit**: Simmering at **110 °C – 140 °C** in Extra Virgin Olive Oil (Picual/Arbequina/Hojiblanca) avoids the acrylamide threshold (> 170 °C) and confits the potato in fatty monounsaturated oleic acid.

## 4. Gastronomic Canon, Master Chefs & National Competitions

### 4.1 Campeonato de España de Tortilla de Patatas
* **Founder & Organizer**: Rafael García Santos (*lomejordelagastronomia.com* / *Alicante Gastronómica*).
* **Champion Chefs & Canonical Locations**:
  * **Alberto García Ponte & Ana María Suárez** (*Mesón O Pote*, Betanzos) — Multiple Spanish Champions (2011, etc.). Pioneers of certified Galician Kennebec and free-range yolks.
  * **Senén González & Ciriaco San Sebastián** (*Restaurante Sagartoki*, Vitoria-Gasteiz) — Champion 2010. Pioneer of caramelized onion balance and frozen gourmet pincho engineering.
  * **Pedro Martino** (*Restaurante L'Ablanu / Naguar*, Oviedo) — Champion 2004.
  * **Itxaso Cisneros** (*Bar Izaro*, Bilbao) — Pioneer of onion-and-sweet-pepper balance.
  * **Cayo Martínez** (*La Casilla*, Betanzos) — Historical cradle of the Betanzos runny style (*sin cebolla*).
  * **Carlos Olabarria** (*La Tortillería*, Vitoria) — Pioneer of potato chip omelettes and high-speed pintxo bar preparation.

## 5. Sociological Surveys & Regional Debates

### 5.1 Centro de Investigaciones Sociológicas (CIS)
* **National Polls**: *Encuestas sobre hábitos gastronómicos y debates culinarios en España* (CIS Estudios No. 3340 y No. 3418, 2021/2023):
  * **The Onion Debate (*Concebollistas vs. Sincebollistas*)**: ~70.4% favor with onion (*con cebolla*), ~20.9% without onion (*sin cebolla*).
  * **Doneness Preferences**: ~53.9% prefer runny / juicy (*poco hecha / jugosa*), ~43.1% prefer firm / well-done (*muy cuajada*).
