### 2.1 Pillar 1: Curated Kitchen Gear (Direct Contextual Affiliate)
Embedded natively within the `/tienda` portal, the Builder interface, and recipe steps:

1. **Non-Stick Skillet Matrix:**
   - **Cast Aluminum Heavy Gauge (Bra Prior, Tefal Unlimited):** High thermal inertia for even heat distribution.
   - **Double-Skillet Turners (*Sartenes Dobles para Tortilla*):** The ultimate tool for beginners fearful of the pan flip.
   - **French Mineral Carbon Steel (De Buyer):** For purist chefs seeking authentic restaurant seasoning.
2. **Food Safety Digital Thermometers:**
   - Instant-read thermocouple probes (Thermapen, ThermoPro) directly recommended whenever recipes reference **70°C for 2 minutes** or **63°C for 20 seconds**.
3. **Japanese Mandolines (Benriner 64mm):**
   - Essential for achieving the consistent 2mm translucent potato wafers mandated by the Betanzos style.
4. **Artisanal Monovarietal Olive Oils:**
   - Curated selections of Spanish AOVE: *Arbequina* (for delicate, buttery profiles) and *Picual* (for robust, high-polyphenol heat stability).
