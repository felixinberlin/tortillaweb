## I. The Empirical Science Lab (Inspired by *Serious Eats / The Food Lab / Modernist Cuisine*)

### 1. The Starch Washing Paradox: Does Cold-Water Potato Rinsing Ruin the Tortilla or Save It?
- **Working Title (ES)**: *La paradoja del almidón: ¿Lavar la patata troceada arruina la ligazón o salva la textura?*
- **Benchmark / Inspiration**: Kenji López-Alt’s *The Potato Lab* (Serious Eats) & Nathan Myhrvold’s *Modernist Cuisine* (Starch Leaching).
- **Priority**: **P0 (Core Culinary Science)**
- **Hook & Thesis**: Conventional culinary wisdom for French fries mandates rinsing sliced potatoes to wash away surface amylose and prevent sticking. In a Spanish tortilla, however, that very surface amylose is the colloidal glue that binds with egg protein. Does rinsing yield greasy, disconnected potato shards, or does it prevent burning during high-heat confit?
- **Empirical Protocol**:
  - 10 identical batches of Kennebec and Monalisa potatoes tested under 4 treatments: unwashed, cold-water rinsed (1 min), warm-water soaked (20 min), and salted-leached.
  - Measurement of oil absorption percentage via digital precision scale.
  - Shear test of finished tortilla slices to quantify structural cohesion.
- **Safety Standard**: Thermal probe validation ensuring internal core reaches **63°C for 20 seconds** (custard) or **70°C for 2 minutes** (safe setting).
- **Knowledge Graph Links**:
  - `ingredients/potato` (Cultivars: Kennebec, Monalisa)
  - `techniques/potato-cutting` (Panadera vs. Chascada)
  - `techniques/warm-emulsion`

### 3. The Salting Chronology: Eggs vs. Potatoes vs. Finishing Flakes
- **Working Title (ES)**: *La cronología de la sal: ¿Al huevo, a la patata caliente o en escamas al emplatar?*
- **Benchmark / Inspiration**: Harold McGee (*On Food and Cooking*, Egg Protein Denaturation Under Saline Pressure).
- **Priority**: **P0 (Fundamental Technique)**
- **Hook & Thesis**: Salting beaten eggs 15 minutes before cooking acts as an anticoagulant, preventing ovalbumin fibers from clumping too tightly and purging water (syneresis). But what happens when salt is applied directly to hot potatoes in the pan, drawing moisture out and dropping oil temperature?
- **Empirical Protocol**:
  - Protocol A: Salt added to raw sliced potatoes 30 min before frying (osmotic water extraction).
  - Protocol B: Salt added to hot potatoes immediately out of the oil.
  - Protocol C: Salt beaten directly into raw eggs prior to the warm soak (*el reposo*).
  - Protocol D: Unsalted cooking with finishing Maldon flakes upon plating.
  - Weeping test: Weigh expelled liquid from cooked wedges after 30 minutes at room temperature.
- **Knowledge Graph Links**:

- **Working Title (ES)**: *El umbral de la salmonela: Cómo servir un centro líquido sin intoxicar a la clientela*
- **Benchmark / Inspiration**: European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), AESAN regulations & *Food Safety Magazine*.

- **Hook & Thesis**: The Spanish obsession with liquid Betanzos-style tortillas directly clashes with Spanish Royal Decree 1021/2022 and health inspection standards.
