# Idea & Vision — tortilladepatatas.org

## 1. Executive Summary

`tortilladepatatas.org` is the definitive open digital encyclopedia, culinary taxonomy, and interactive "Kitchen Notebook" dedicated to the Spanish Omelette (*tortilla de patatas* / *tortilla española*). 

The platform bridges culinary passion, historical debate, and rigorous food safety science. It transforms what is traditionally a casual debate—onion vs. no onion, runny vs. set, garlic vs. peppers—into an organized, taxonomy-driven digital experience.

---

## 2. Core Pillars & Philosophy

### A. The Culinary Spectrum (Factions & Dogmas)
In Spanish gastronomy, the tortilla de patatas is not merely a recipe; it is a cultural movement with distinct factions:
* **Los Puristas**: Egg, potato, oil, salt. Nothing else. Clean, timeless, unadulterated.
* **Los Concebollistas**: Slow-caramelized onions providing natural sweetness and moisture.
* **Los Pimientistas**: Roasted or fried green/red peppers adding vegetal warmth and aroma.
* **Los Ajistas**: Subtle garlic infusion elevating the potato fry.
* **Los 'Con Cosas'**: Modernist and innovative variations (potato chips, chorizo, cheese, mushrooms).

### B. Food Safety Science as a First Principle
While respecting regional preferences for runny (*jugosa*) vs. firm (*cuajada*) textures, `tortilladepatatas.org` embeds non-negotiable food safety guidance:
* **Gold Pasteurization Standard**: **70°C for 2 minutes** to eliminate *Salmonella* and bacteria completely.
* **Safe Runny Yolk Standard**: **63°C for 20 seconds** when preparing juicy or soft-centered tortillas.
* **Ambient Threshold**: Maximum **4 hours** at room temperature before mandatory refrigeration (**<8°C**).

### C. Skeuomorphic-Modernist "Kitchen Notebook" Design
The application embraces a warm, tactile aesthetic inspired by heirloom handwritten chef journals, pairing tactile parchment backgrounds (`#F5E6BE`), yolk gold accents (`#FFB800`), and caramelized umber borders (`#8D6E63`) with responsive modern typography and interactive tools.

## 3. Key Interactive Features

1. **Taxonomy & Faction Profiles**:
   * Interactive faction explorer with full-width hero imagery, philosophy breakdown, key ingredients, prominent figures, and linked recipes.
2. **Interactive Recipe & DNA Builder**:
   * Dynamic potato-to-egg ratio calculator, juiciness estimator, and step-by-step cooking guide.
3. **Faction Matrix & Comparator**:
   * Side-by-side comparison of ingredients, techniques, and philosophical dogmas.
4. **Food Safety Monitor**:
   * Temperature & exposure validator providing real-time risk status (Safe, Caution, High Risk).
5. **Multilingual Localization**:
   * Native, full-locale support for Spanish (`es`), English (`en`), and German (`de`).
