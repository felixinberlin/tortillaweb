# The Open Flame of Chamberí: Pepe's Castizo Madrid Tavern

At seven-thirty in the morning, while Madrid's streetlights still cast long amber glows across the sidewalks of Chamberí, the rich aroma of pure olive oil and sizzling potatoes already billows from Pepe's corner tavern. In this neighborhood institution founded in 1928 by his grandfather, there are no QR codes or white tablecloths. Across the hammered zinc bar, over sixty steaming tortillas emerge fresh each morning, consumed in hearty wedges by taxi drivers, builders, bank tellers, and students.

## The Shrine of Seasoned Cast Iron & Open Flame

"A seasoned cast iron skillet has memory," Pepe declares, lifting a jet-black heirloom pan polished by over one hundred thousand tortillas. "Modern non-stick coatings cannot handle the searing thermal blast needed to flash-set a melty, golden tortilla. We work over open flame: thirty seconds per side to form a crisp skin while keeping the heart silky as custard."

Pepe's tavern craftsmanship follows three unbending principles:
1. **Rhythmic frying of Agria potatoes**: Medium-diced [potatoes](/en/ingredients/potato) simmered in pure [extra virgin olive oil](/en/ingredients/olive-oil), finished with high heat to crisp the edges while keeping the core fluffy.
2. **Generous egg ratio**: Eight large free-range [eggs](/en/ingredients/egg) per kilogram of potatoes, adhering proudly to Spain's [purist tradition](/en/factions/purists).
3. **The decisive wrist snap**: Two swift, assertive flips with a heavy earthenware plate. Hesitation when turning is the mother of all kitchen disasters.

## Food Safety & Tavern Rigor in Historic Madrid

Despite the relentless morning rush in [Madrid](/en/regions/madrid), Pepe is uncompromising regarding microbiological safety. His beloved, runny [Betanzos Style Tortilla](/en/recipes/betanzos) relies on rapid heat conduction to ensure the egg-potato blend safely reaches **70°C for 2 minutes** (or **63°C for 20 seconds**).

"On my counter, no freshly prepared tortilla is ever exposed beyond the safe **4 hours** limit," Pepe notes with a grin. "Though in truth, they rarely last twenty minutes before being devoured to the last crumb. Any batch stored between meal services is strictly refrigerated below **8°C**."

## The Heartbeat of the Neighborhood

"A tavern tortilla doesn't seek Michelin plaques or social media fame; it feeds the hardworking spirit of the neighborhood. A warm slice, crusty rustic bread, and a perfectly drawn caña: that is the true soul of Madrid."
